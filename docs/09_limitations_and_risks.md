# 09 — Limitations and Risks

- **Not investment advice; not for live trading.** Historical simulation only.
- Cost assumptions (0.15% + 0.05%) are estimates; real slippage/fees vary.
- Macro period-vs-publication dates are approximated (conservative next-month rule).
- News files may include post-close articles; same-day treatment is an assumption.
- t+5 labels overlap; embargo mitigates but non-stationarity remains.
- ~1908 samples / 68 macro obs — small for deep multimodal learning.
- Momentum-5d strength is in-sample test-slice observation, not a validated strategy.
- No shorting, no position sizing, no regime risk controls modeled.
